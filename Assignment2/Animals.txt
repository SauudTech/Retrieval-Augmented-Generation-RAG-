Animals live in almost every part of the world, from hot deserts and dense forests to freezing polar regions and deep oceans. Scientists group them by what they eat, where they live, and how they raise their young, and they have already named more than one million animal species.

The elephant is the largest land animal in the world, and an adult African elephant can weigh up to 6,000 kilograms. Elephants live in herds led by the oldest female, and they use their long trunks to drink, eat, and smell.

The blue whale is the largest animal that has ever lived on Earth, reaching up to 30 meters in length. Although it is gigantic, it eats only tiny shrimp-like creatures called krill.

The cheetah is the fastest land animal, able to run at about 110 kilometers per hour in short bursts. It lives mainly in the grasslands of Africa and uses its speed to catch gazelles.

The giraffe is the tallest animal in the world, with some adults reaching about 5 meters in height. Its long neck helps it eat leaves from the tops of trees that other animals cannot reach.

The lion is often called the king of the jungle, although it actually lives in grasslands and savannas. Lions are the only big cats that live in groups, called prides, and the females do most of the hunting.

The camel is well known as the ship of the desert because it can travel for many days without drinking water. It stores fat in its hump, and its wide feet help it walk on soft sand.

The penguin is a bird that cannot fly but is an excellent swimmer. Most penguins live in the cold regions of the Southern Hemisphere, and the emperor penguin is the tallest and heaviest species.

The dolphin is a very intelligent sea mammal that lives in groups called pods. Dolphins communicate with clicks and whistles, and they breathe air through a hole on top of their heads.

The honey bee is a small insect that plays a very important role in nature by pollinating flowers and crops. A single hive can contain tens of thousands of bees, and they make honey from the nectar of flowers.
