Football clubs around the world are compared mostly by the number of major trophies they have won, including domestic league titles, national cups, and international competitions. Some clubs have built huge histories of success over more than a century.

Real Madrid from Spain is the most successful club in the history of the UEFA Champions League, with 15 European Cup titles. The club has also won the Spanish La Liga more than 35 times, which is a record in Spain.

FC Barcelona, also from Spain, has won the Champions League 5 times and La Liga around 28 times. The club is famous for its youth academy La Masia, which produced stars such as Lionel Messi, Xavi, and Iniesta.

Bayern Munich is the most successful club in Germany, with more than 30 Bundesliga titles and 6 European Cup titles. The club has dominated German football for many years.

AC Milan from Italy has won the European Cup 7 times, making it one of the most decorated clubs in Europe. The club is also a famous name in Serie A history, with 19 Italian league titles.

Liverpool from England has won the European Cup 6 times, which is the most for an English club. The club has also won the English league title 20 times, equal to the record of Manchester United.

Manchester United has won the English league title 20 times and the European Cup 3 times. The club became famous under manager Sir Alex Ferguson, who won 13 Premier League titles with the team.

Juventus is the most successful club in Italy, with more than 35 Serie A titles. The club has reached the Champions League final many times, but has won the competition only 2 times.

Al Hilal is the most successful club in Saudi Arabia, with around 19 Saudi league titles and several Asian Champions League trophies. The club is based in Riyadh and has a very large fan base across the country.

Al Ahly from Egypt is the most successful club in Africa, with 12 CAF Champions League titles and more than 40 Egyptian league titles. It is often called the club of the century in Africa.
