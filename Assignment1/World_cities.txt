The largest cities in the world are usually measured by urban agglomeration, which counts the whole built-up area instead of only the official city limits. Most of the biggest cities are in Asia, and rankings change as populations grow and statistical methods are updated.

Jakarta, the capital of Indonesia, is ranked by recent United Nations estimates as the most populous urban area in the world, with roughly 42 million people. It is located on the northwest coast of Java island and suffers from heavy traffic and sinking land.

Dhaka, the capital of Bangladesh, has about 37 million residents and is one of the most densely populated cities on Earth. It is famous for its rickshaws, textile industry, and busy markets.

Tokyo, the capital of Japan, has about 33 million people in its greater metropolitan area and was the largest city in the world for many decades. It is a global center of finance, technology, and culture.

New Delhi and the wider Delhi urban area in India have around 30 million inhabitants. Delhi is the political center of India and faces serious challenges such as air pollution and water supply.

Shanghai, in China, has nearly 30 million people and is the largest city in the country. It is a major financial hub with one of the busiest container ports in the world.

Cairo, the capital of Egypt, has roughly 26 million people and is the largest city in Africa and the Arab world. It lies along the Nile River near the pyramids of Giza.

Mexico City, the capital of Mexico, has about 22 million people and is the largest city in North America by metropolitan population. It was built on top of the ancient Aztec capital of Tenochtitlan.

Sao Paulo, in Brazil, has around 22 million inhabitants and is the largest city in South America. It is the financial capital of Brazil and is known for its diverse food and culture.

Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia, has more than 7 million people and is one of the fastest growing cities in the Middle East. The city is a center of government and business, and it is expected to keep growing quickly in the coming years.
